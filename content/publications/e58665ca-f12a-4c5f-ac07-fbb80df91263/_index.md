    hopes for diagnostic support for psychiatric patients based on brain MRI. But while
    technical advances are undoubtedly remarkable, the current trajectory of mostly
    proof-of-concept studies performed on retrospective, often repository-derived data,
    may not be well suited to yield a substantial impact in clinical practice. Here
    we review these developments and challenges, arguing for the need of stronger involvement
    of and input from medical doctors in order to pave the way for machine-learning
    in clinical psychiatry.
