  description: "Software development and data curation or analysis share many of their\
    \ issues: keeping track of the evolution of files \u2013 ideally with information\
    \ on how, why, when, and by whom \u2013 , organizing collaboration with multiple\
    \ people, keeping track of known issues and other TODOs, discussing changes, making\
    \ versions available to others, automating tasks, and more. Often you will even\
    \ have to write code as part of a data project, blurring the line between the two\
    \ even more. In the free and open-source software development world these issues\
    \ already have well established solutions: a version control system keeps track\
    \ of your projects history and ongoing development, a forge can serve as a collaboration\
    \ hub, CI/CD services provide flexible automation. So, why not apply them to our\
    \ data management needs? Git, git-annex and DataLad together build the foundation\
    \ of a decentralized version control system for large datasets, with DataLad being\
    \ specifically tailored towards Research Data Management. Forgejo-aneksajo on the\
    \ other hand extends Forgejo \u2013 a well-established free software git forge \u2013\
    \ with git-annex support, turning it into a versatile self-hostable (meta-)data\
    \ collaboration hub for this ecosystem."
