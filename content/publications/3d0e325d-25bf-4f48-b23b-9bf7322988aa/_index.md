  description: "INTRODUCTIONHippocampal local and network dysfunction is the hallmark\
    \ of Alzheimer's disease (AD).METHODSWe characterized the spatial patterns of hippocampus\
    \ differentiation based on brain co-metabolism in healthy elderly participants and\
    \ demonstrated their relevance to study local metabolic changes and associated dysfunction\
    \ in pathological aging.RESULTSThe hippocampus can be differentiated into anterior/posterior\
    \ and dorsal cornu ammonis (CA)/ventral (subiculum) subregions. While anterior/posterior\
    \ CA show co-metabolism with different regions of the subcortical limbic networks,\
    \ the anterior/posterior subiculum are parts of cortical networks supporting object-centered\
    \ memory and higher cognitive demands, respectively. Both networks show relationships\
    \ with the spatial patterns of gene expression pertaining to cell energy metabolism\
    \ and AD's process. Finally, while local metabolism is generally lower in posterior\
    \ regions, the anterior\u2013posterior imbalance is maximal in late mild cognitive\
    \ impairment with the anterior subiculum being relatively preserved.DISCUSSIONFuture\
    \ studies should consider bidimensional hippocampal differentiation and in particular\
    \ the posterior subicular region to better understand pathological aging."
