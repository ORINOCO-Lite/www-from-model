    \ to investigate neurobiological changes in emotion processing in patients with\

    \ imaging (fMRI).Methods20 patients with ID and 20 HC were included in this study\

    \ T0, whereas no significant difference was observed between the groups at T1. However,\
    \ the analysis of Intraclass Correlation Coefficients (ICC) in the control group\

    \ may normalise amygdala responses to sleep-related negative stimuli, which may\

    \ reliability of fMRI measures warrants cautious interpretation of these results."
