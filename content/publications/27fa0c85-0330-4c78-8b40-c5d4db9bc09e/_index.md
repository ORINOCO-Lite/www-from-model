  description: "Working memory (WM) is defined as the ability to maintain a representation\
    \ online to guide goal\u2010directed behavior. Its capacity in early childhood predicts\
    \ academic achievements in late childhood and its deficits are found in various\
    \ neurodevelopmental disorders. We employed resting\u2010state fMRI (rs\u2010fMRI)\
    \ of 468 participants aged from 4 to 55\u2009years and connectome\u2010based predictive\
    \ modeling (CPM) to explore the potential predictive power of intrinsic functional\
    \ networks to WM in preschoolers, early and late school\u2010age children, adolescents,\
    \ and adults. We defined intrinsic functional networks among brain regions identified\
    \ by activation likelihood estimation (ALE) meta\u2010analysis on existing WM functional\
    \ studies (ALE\u2010based intrinsic functional networks) and intrinsic functional\
    \ networks generated based on the whole brain (whole\u2010brain intrinsic functional\
    \ networks). We employed the CPM on these networks to predict WM in each age group.\
    \ The CPM using the ALE\u2010based and whole\u2010brain intrinsic functional networks\
    \ predicted WM of individual adults, while the prediction power of the ALE\u2010\
    based intrinsic functional networks was superior to that of the whole\u2010brain\
    \ intrinsic functional networks. Nevertheless, the CPM using the whole\u2010brain\
    \ but not the ALE\u2010based intrinsic functional networks predicted WM in adolescents.\
    \ And, the CPM using neither the ALE\u2010based nor whole\u2010brain networks predicted\
    \ WM in any of the children groups. Our findings showed the trend of the prediction\
    \ power of the intrinsic functional networks to cognition in individuals from early\
    \ childhood to adulthood."
