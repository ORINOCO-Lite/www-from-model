  description: "Prosocial behaviors are hypothesized to require socio-cognitive and\
    \ empathic abilities\u2014engaging brain regions attributed to the mentalizing and\
    \ empathy brain networks. Here, we tested this hypothesis with a coordinate-based\
    \ meta-analysis of 600 neuroimaging studies on prosociality, mentalizing and empathy\
    \ (\u223C12,000 individuals). We showed that brain areas recruited by prosocial\
    \ behaviors only partially overlap with the mentalizing (dorsal posterior cingulate\
    \ cortex) and empathy networks (middle cingulate cortex). Additionally, the dorsolateral\
    \ and ventromedial prefrontal cortices were preferentially activated by prosocial\
    \ behaviors. Analyses on the functional connectivity profile and functional roles\
    \ of the neural patterns underlying prosociality revealed that in addition to socio-cognitive\
    \ and empathic processes, prosocial behaviors further involve evaluation processes\
    \ and action planning, likely to select the action sequence that best satisfies\
    \ another person\u2019s needs. By characterizing the multidimensional construct\
    \ of prosociality at the neural level, we provide insights that may support a better\
    \ understanding of normal and abnormal social cognition (e.g., psychopathy)."
