Interdisciplinary field that studies how people (and other intelligent systems) perceive, learn, think, understand language, and make decisions. It draws from psychology, neuroscience, linguistics, computer science, philosophy, and anthropology, aiming to explain the mind using methods like experiments, brain imaging, and computational models.
